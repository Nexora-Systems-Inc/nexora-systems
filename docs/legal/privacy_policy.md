## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **CrewPilot**, a workforce management application developed and operated by **Nexora Systems** ("Nexora", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you regarding your personal and business information when you use CrewPilot.

CrewPilot is designed to help organizations manage workforce operations, including employee accounts, projects, time tracking, payroll calculations, estimates, materials, reporting, and other business activities. To provide these services, we collect and process only the information reasonably necessary to operate the platform securely and reliably.

Your privacy is important to us. We are committed to handling your information responsibly, protecting it using industry-standard security practices, and being transparent about how your information is collected and used.

By creating an account, accessing, or using CrewPilot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not use the application.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of CrewPilot, including organization owners, administrators, managers, employees, invited users, and other authorized individuals who access the platform through an organization.

## 2. Information We Collect

To provide CrewPilot's workforce management services, we collect and process information that is necessary to operate the platform, secure user accounts, and support the organizations using our services.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create or access a CrewPilot account, we may collect information such as:

This information is used solely to provide the operational features requested by your organization.

- Browser type
- Device type

CrewPilot may offer optional voice-enabled features, including interactions with Ashley, our AI assistant.

### 2.6 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

CrewPilot is designed to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide workforce management services.

We do not intentionally:

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Protecting your information is a core responsibility of Nexora Systems.

CrewPilot is designed with security, reliability, and responsible data handling in mind. While no technology platform can guarantee absolute security, we take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect the information entrusted to us.

### 4.1 Secure Infrastructure

CrewPilot stores business and operational data using secure cloud infrastructure and trusted service providers.

Data transmitted between your device and our services is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

---

### 4.2 Authentication and Access Control

Access to CrewPilot is protected through secure authentication mechanisms.

Depending on your organization, users may authenticate using:

- Email and password
- Google Sign-In
- GitHub Sign-In

Access to information within CrewPilot is controlled through role-based permissions. Users are only permitted to access information appropriate for their assigned role within their organization.

---

### 4.3 Organization Data Isolation

CrewPilot is designed so that each organization's information remains logically separated from other organizations using the platform.

We continuously improve our security controls to help ensure that users can only access data belonging to organizations they are authorized to access.

---

### 4.4 Offline Data Storage

To support offline functionality, CrewPilot may temporarily store certain operational information on your device.

This locally stored information exists solely to provide offline capabilities and synchronize your work when connectivity is restored.

Offline information is not intended to be permanently stored on your device and is managed by the application as part of its normal operation.

---

### 4.5 Data Retention and Backups

Operational data may be retained for backup, recovery, audit, and business continuity purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on legal requirements, contractual obligations, and the operational needs of the organizations using CrewPilot.

---

### 4.6 Your Responsibilities

Security is a shared responsibility.

Users are responsible for:

- Protecting their account credentials.
- Maintaining the security of devices used to access CrewPilot.
- Reporting suspected unauthorized access promptly.
- Using the application in accordance with their organization's security policies.

---

### 4.7 Security Improvements

Nexora Systems continually evaluates and improves the security of CrewPilot.

As the platform evolves, security controls, monitoring capabilities, and protective measures may be updated to address new technologies, emerging threats, and industry best practices.

You may request access to the personal information associated with your CrewPilot account.

When information is no longer required, Nexora Systems will take reasonable steps to securely remove, anonymize, or dispose of information in accordance with applicable legal requirements and industry practices.
