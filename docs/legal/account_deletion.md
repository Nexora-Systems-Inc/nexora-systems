This Account Deletion Policy explains how users may request deletion of a CrewPilot account and what happens to personal and organizational information when an account is removed.

---

## 1. Introduction

This Account Deletion Policy explains how Nexora Systems handles requests to delete CrewPilot user accounts.

It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](./privacy_policy.md) and [Terms of Service](./terms_of_service.md), which describe how we collect, use, retain, and protect information more broadly.

CrewPilot is a workforce management platform used by organizations to manage employees, projects, time tracking, payroll, estimates, materials, invoicing, and related business operations. Because operational records may belong to the organization using the platform, account deletion does not always mean that all information associated with a user's work history is removed.

This policy is designed to be transparent about what account deletion means, what information may be deleted, and what information may be retained.

---

## 2. Requesting Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their CrewPilot account by contacting Nexora Systems using the contact information in Section 9 of this policy.

To help us process your request efficiently, please include **"Account Deletion Request"** in the subject line of your email and provide:

- The email address associated with your CrewPilot account
- The name of the organization or organizations you accessed through CrewPilot, if known
- Any additional information that may help us verify your identity and locate your account

**Current process:** Account deletion is handled through email request. CrewPilot does not currently provide an in-app self-service account deletion feature in the production release.

Organizations may also remove a user's access to their CrewPilot workspace through organization administration tools. Removing a user from an organization is not the same as deleting the user's CrewPilot account. If you want your personal account deleted, you must submit a request to Nexora Systems as described above.

---

## 3. What Happens When an Account Is Deleted

When Nexora Systems processes an approved account deletion request, account deletion generally means:

- The user's ability to sign in to CrewPilot using that account is disabled or removed
- Authentication credentials associated with the account are removed or deactivated where appropriate
- Personal account information, such as the user's profile and membership association, is removed or anonymized where practical
- The user will no longer be able to access organizations, projects, or operational records through that account

Account deletion is intended to end the individual user's access to CrewPilot. It does not delete an entire organization or remove all operational records created by that user while working on behalf of an organization.

Nexora Systems will make reasonable efforts to remove or anonymize personal information associated with the deleted account, subject to the retention requirements described in Sections 4 and 5 of this policy.

---

## 4. Organization-Owned Business Records

Information created while working within an organization may belong to that organization rather than the individual user.

Operational records created through CrewPilot may continue to be retained by the organization even after an individual account has been removed. Examples include:

- Time entries
- Break records
- Payroll calculations
- Project history
- Client records
- Estimates
- Invoices
- Materials
- Work descriptions
- Activity history

These records may be retained where necessary for legitimate business operations, accounting, payroll, auditing, contractual obligations, or applicable law.

Organizations are responsible for managing their workforce records and determining how retained operational information is used in accordance with their own policies and legal obligations.

Deleting a personal CrewPilot account does not transfer ownership of organization business records to the individual user.

---

## 5. Information That May Be Retained

Even after an account deletion request is processed, certain information may continue to exist for limited and legitimate purposes, including:

- Legal compliance
- Payroll and employment record requirements
- Accounting and tax obligations
- Fraud prevention and security investigations
- Enforcing our Terms of Service
- Resolving disputes
- Disaster recovery
- Backup restoration
- Business continuity

Retention is limited to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes. When information is no longer required, Nexora Systems will take reasonable steps to securely remove, anonymize, or dispose of it in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our Privacy Policy.

Backup copies of information may exist for a limited period as part of normal system recovery processes before being removed according to our retention practices.

---

## 6. Processing Timeline

Nexora Systems will make reasonable efforts to process account deletion requests within thirty (30) days of receiving a verified request, or sooner where required by applicable law.

Some requests may take longer to complete if additional verification is required, if the account is associated with multiple organizations, or if legal or operational review is necessary.

We will contact you if we need additional information to process your request.

---

## 7. Identity Verification

To protect customer information and organizational security, Nexora Systems may verify the identity of the person submitting an account deletion request before processing it.

Verification may include confirming account details, email address ownership, organization association, or other reasonable information related to the request.

We will not delete an account until we are reasonably satisfied that the request is legitimate and submitted by the account holder or an authorized representative.

---

## 8. Future Self-Service Deletion

Future versions of CrewPilot may include self-service account deletion tools within the application.

Until such functionality is implemented and announced, account deletion requests must be submitted by email using the contact information in Section 9 of this policy.

When self-service deletion becomes available, this policy will be updated to describe the in-app process and any differences in how requests are handled.
